🚀 Backend Storage Validator

⚠️ Before You Start:

Ensure you have configured the Volume in Railway:

  1. Go to Railway Dashboard
  2. Click your booking-backend service
  3. Go to Volumes tab -> New Volume -> Mount Path: /app/uploads
  4. Go to Variables tab -> Add STORAGE_BASE_PATH = /app/uploads
STEP 1: LOGIN

Enter your app credentials to get an access token.

STEP 2: CREATE TEST LISTING

This will create a new listing with a small test image to verify persistence.
