# Icon Design Brief for Apartify Africa

## For Designers (Fiverr/99designs)

### Project Overview
Create a unique app icon for "Apartify Africa" - a Nigerian apartment rental platform. The current icon was rejected by Apple for being too generic (4.1.0 Copycats guideline).

---

## Requirements

### Technical Specs
- **Size:** 1024x1024 pixels (exact)
- **Format:** PNG
- **Background:** Solid color (no transparency)
- **File Size:** Under 500KB
- **Style:** Simple, modern, works at 60x60px

### Brand Colors
- **Primary:** Gold (#FFD700)
- **Background:** White (#FFFFFF) or Gold (#FFD700)
- **Accent:** Dark gray/black (#333333) for contrast

### Design Concept
**Main Idea:** Modern apartment building with integrated key element

**Elements:**
1. Apartment building silhouette (3-4 stories, modern/rectangular)
2. House key integrated as door or window
3. Simple, bold design
4. Optional: Subtle African/Nigerian design element (geometric pattern)

### Design Style
- **Simple:** Bold shapes, minimal details
- **Modern:** Clean lines, contemporary look
- **Distinctive:** Unique combination not seen in other apps
- **Scalable:** Recognizable at small sizes

---

## What We Want

### ✅ Must Have
- Apartment/housing representation
- Key element (represents access/rental)
- Gold color (#FFD700)
- Simple, bold design
- Unique (not generic)

### ✅ Should Have
- Modern building silhouette
- Clean, professional look
- Works at small sizes
- Distinctive from competitors

### ✅ Nice to Have
- Subtle African/Nigerian design element
- Geometric pattern
- Contemporary styling

---

## What to Avoid

### ❌ Don't Use
- Generic "AA" letters (current rejected design)
- Simple house icon alone (too common)
- Generic key icon alone
- Designs similar to Airbnb, Booking.com, etc.
- Stock icons without customization
- Text-heavy designs
- Complex patterns with fine details
- Designs that look like other rental apps

### ❌ Avoid Similarity To
- Airbnb (house icon)
- Booking.com (booking symbol)
- Generic real estate apps
- Simple letter-based icons
