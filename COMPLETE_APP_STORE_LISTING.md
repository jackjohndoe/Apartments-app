# Complete App Store Listing - Step by Step Guide

## Current Status

## Required Fields to Complete

### 1. Screenshots (MANDATORY - Minimum 3 per device size)

**Location:** On the App Store listing page, scroll to "Preview and Screenshots" section

**Required Sizes:**
- **iPhone 6.7" (iPhone 14 Pro Max):** 1290 x 2796 pixels - Minimum 3 screenshots
- **iPhone 6.5" (iPhone 11 Pro Max):** 1242 x 2688 pixels - Minimum 3 screenshots

**How to Add:**
1. Click on the "iPhone" tab in the screenshots section
2. Click "View All Sizes in Media Manager" link
3. Upload screenshots showing:
   - Home/Explore screen with apartment listings
   - Apartment details page
   - Booking flow
   - Wallet/payment features
   - Profile screen

**Note:** Screenshots are REQUIRED - you cannot submit without them.

### 2. App Description & Subtitle

**Location:** Scroll down on the App Store listing page

**Required Fields:**
- **Subtitle** (30 characters max): Brief tagline for your app
- **Description** (4000 characters max): Full description of your app

**Suggested Content:**
- **Subtitle:** "Find & Book Apartments in Nigeria"
- **Description:** 
  ```
  Apartify Africa is your one-stop platform for finding and booking apartments across Nigeria. 
  
  Features:
  - Browse thousands of verified apartment listings
  - Real-time availability and pricing
  - Secure in-app booking and payments
  - Virtual account funding for seamless transactions
  - User-friendly interface for easy navigation
  
  Whether you're looking for a short-term stay or long-term rental, Apartify Africa makes finding your perfect home simple and secure.
  ```

### 3. Keywords (100 characters max)

**Location:** On the App Store listing page, find the "Keyword" field

**Suggested Keywords:**
```
apartments, rental, Nigeria, booking, accommodation, housing, property, rent, Lagos, Abuja
```

### 4. Support URL (REQUIRED)

**Location:** On the App Store listing page, find the "Support URL" field

**Required:** Must be a publicly accessible URL

**Options:**
- Your website support page: `https://yourwebsite.com/support`
- Email link: `mailto:support@yourwebsite.com`
- GitHub issues page (if open source)
- Create a simple support page on your website

**Example:** `https://apartifyafrica.com/support` or `mailto:support@apartifyafrica.com`

### 5. Marketing URL (Optional but Recommended)

**Location:** On the App Store listing page, find the "Marketing URL" field

**Suggested:** Your main website URL
**Example:** `https://apartifyafrica.com`

### 6. Privacy Policy URL (REQUIRED)

**Location:** Go to "App Information" → Find "Privacy Policy URL"

**Steps:**
1. Click on "General" in the left sidebar
2. Click "App Information"
3. Find "Privacy Policy URL" field
4. Enter a publicly accessible privacy policy URL

**Required:** Must be publicly accessible

**Options:**
- Create a privacy policy page on your website
- Use a privacy policy generator
- Host on GitHub Pages

**Example:** `https://apartifyafrica.com/privacy-policy`

### 7. Build Selection (REQUIRED)

**Location:** On the App Store listing page, scroll to "Build" section

**Steps:**
1. Click "Add Build" button
2. Select your build from TestFlight (should show build 1.0.0 (12))
3. If no builds appear:
   - Go to TestFlight section
   - Verify the build is processed
   - Wait for processing to complete (can take 10-30 minutes)

**Note:** The build must be in "Ready to Submit" status in TestFlight before it can be selected.

### 8. App Review Information

**Location:** Scroll down to "Sign-In Information" section

**Required if your app requires sign-in:**
- **Username:** Test account username
- **Password:** Test account password
- **Contact Information:** Your contact details for Apple reviewers
- **Notes:** Any special instructions for reviewers

**If your app doesn't require sign-in:**
- Uncheck "Sign-in required" checkbox

### 9. Version Information

**Location:** Scroll to "Version Information" section

**Fields:**
- **Version:** Already set to "1.0"
- **What's New in This Version:** Release notes (optional for first version)

**Suggested "What's New":**
```
Initial release of Apartify Africa. Find and book apartments across Nigeria with ease.
```

2. Review all sections to ensure nothing is missing
3. Click "Add for Review" button
4. Answer any export compliance questions
5. Submit for review
